OSA Severity Scale

AHI (Apnea-Hypopnea Index) is the key metric used to classify sleep apnea severity.

Normal
AHI < 5

No significant sleep apnea. Healthy sleep breathing patterns.

Mild
AHI 5–14

Mild OSA. Lifestyle changes and monitoring recommended.

Moderate
AHI 15–29

Moderate OSA. CPAP therapy typically recommended.

Severe
AHI ≥ 30

Severe OSA. Immediate medical consultation required.
